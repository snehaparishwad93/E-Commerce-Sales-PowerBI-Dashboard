# E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard – Power BI 📊

An interactive E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard created using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales, costs, profitability, products, customer segments, and regional performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow, from data cleaning and transformation to data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard creation.

The dashboard provides a clear view of business performance and helps identify important sales, profit, product, customer, and regional trends.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Track monthly sales performance
- Identify profitable products and categories
- Analyze customer segment contribution
- Identify top revenue-generating products
- Compare regional order performance
- Create an interactive and meaningful business dashboard

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Financial Performance: Generated approximately 30M in total sales, 23M in costs, and 7M in total profit, resulting in a 22% profit margin.
- Monthly Sales Trend: Sales reached the highest level in May and dropped significantly in September.
- Product Profitability: Electronics and Computers are the strongest contributors to profitability.
- Top Revenue Product: Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 generated the highest revenue among the displayed products.
- Customer Segments: The Consumer segment contributes the largest share across the major categories.
- Regional Orders: Mumbai has the highest order volume among the displayed regions.
- The dashboard also provides insights into category performance, product sales volume, customer-wise profit, and regional order distribution.

## 🧮 DAX Measures

Key DAX measures created for the dashboard include:

- Total Sales – Calculates the total sales generated.
- Total Cost – Calculates the total cost.
- Total Profit – Calculates total profit.
- Profit Margin – Calculates profit as a percentage of total sales.
- Total Orders – Calculates the number of unique orders.
- Total Quantity – Calculates the total quantity sold.

These measures were used to create KPI cards and support the dashboard analysis.
